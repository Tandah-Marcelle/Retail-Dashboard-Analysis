## 2. Technology Category

### The Problem
Technology (Computers, Monitors, Accessories) generates the most revenue — approximately 26.5 billion FCFA net over the analysis period. However, it also attracted the largest absolute discounts (~5.5 billion FCFA given away), driven specifically by:
- **PRD-201 — Enterprise Laptop 15"** (unit price: 480,000 FCFA): High-ticket item, heavy promotional activity
- **PRD-202 — 27-inch 4K Monitor** (unit price: 175,000 FCFA): Second-most discounted item

The sub-category breakdown from the dashboard shows Computers at ~37% margin and Monitors at ~37% — both significantly below the company average and the lowest performers in the entire catalog.

### Why This Happens
High unit prices create a psychological temptation for sales teams to offer large nominal discounts that "feel" small in percentage terms. A 20% discount on a 480,000 FCFA laptop is 96,000 FCFA — handed away per transaction.

### Recommendations for Technology

**R-T1: Protect Accessories — They Don't Need Discounting**
Accessories (Headsets, PRD-203) maintain a margin near 57% — the highest in the sub-category breakdown. They require zero deep discounting. Cap Accessories discounts at 8% maximum.

**R-T2: Redesign the Laptop & Monitor Pricing Approach**
Rather than offering percentage discounts on the Enterprise Laptop, introduce:
- Bundled pricing (Laptop + Headset or Laptop + Monitor at a fixed package price)
- Trade-in credits that feel like value without reducing per-unit margin
- Loyalty-gated pricing: only returning Corporate customers qualify for >10% discounts

**R-T3: Set Technology Discount Hard Cap at 15%**
Based on the profitability concentration chart, Technology transactions above 15% discount contribute near-zero marginal profit. 15% is the ceiling.

**R-T4: Monitor Technology Quarterly — It Leads the Decline**
Given Technology's dominance in revenue share, its margin performance should be reported at every quarterly review as a standalone KPI.

## 3. Furniture Category

### The Problem
Furniture is the second most revenue-generating category (~13 billion FCFA net) and has the second-highest discount exposure. The key affected products:
- **PRD-101 — Ergonomic Office Chair** (unit price: 95,000 FCFA): Volume product, heavily discounted
- **PRD-102 — Executive Wooden Desk** (unit price: 180,000 FCFA): High cost item with thin margin window

Furniture also exhibits the sharpest margin degradation in the Margin Degradation Across Discount Bands chart — it falls faster than Technology and Office Supplies as discount rates increase.

### The Cost Structure Reality
```
Executive Wooden Desk (PRD-102):
  Standard unit cost:  85,000 FCFA
  Standard unit price: 180,000 FCFA
  Gross margin:        95,000 FCFA (52.8% at full price)

At 20% discount:
  Net revenue:    144,000 FCFA
  Net profit:      59,000 FCFA
  Actual margin:   41.0%

At 30% discount:
  Net revenue:    126,000 FCFA
  Net profit:      41,000 FCFA
  Actual margin:   32.5%
```

Every 10 percentage points of additional discount costs roughly 8–9 margin points on Furniture.

### Recommendations for Furniture

**R-F1: Reposition Furniture as a Premium Category**
Chairs and Desks are high-consideration purchases. Customers are not primarily price-sensitive on these products — they are quality and durability-sensitive. Sales training should shift focus from price negotiation to value communication (ergonomics, lifespan, productivity).

**R-F2: Introduce Clearance Windows Instead of Ongoing Discounts**
Rather than allowing ongoing deep discounts across the year, run defined quarterly clearance windows (max 2 weeks per quarter) for end-of-line or slow-moving SKUs. Outside of these windows, Furniture discounts should not exceed 12%.

**R-F3: Launch a Corporate Furniture Package**
Corporate customers (30% of the customer base) are likely the primary buyers of Desks and Chairs. Offer pre-negotiated corporate contracts with fixed pricing (e.g., 10% off for 20+ units) rather than letting sales reps discount ad hoc.

**R-F4: Review Furnishings Sub-Category**
PRD-103 (Standing Desk Converter, unit price: 65,000 FCFA) is classified under Furnishings. It appears in the dashboard as relatively stable. Ensure Furnishings maintains its current performance by capping discounts at 10%.

## 4. Office Supplies Category

### The Strength
Office Supplies (Paper & Ink, Binders, Accessories) is NorthPeak's most margin-efficient category at ~50%. It generates the least revenue (~3.6 billion FCFA net) but requires the least effort to maintain.

Key performers:
- **Accessories: ~57% margin** — highest in the entire catalog
- **Binders: ~55% margin** — second highest
- **Paper & Ink: ~48% margin** — solid

### The Risk
Because Office Supplies is a low-cost, high-frequency purchase category, it can be vulnerable to competitive undercutting. If competitors begin offering significant discounts on paper, toner, and staples, NorthPeak may feel pressure to match.

### Recommendations for Office Supplies

**R-O1: Do Not Over-Discount — There Is No Business Case**
Office Supplies already sell at low price points. Discounting them does not meaningfully increase volume (r = 0.00 between quantity and discount). Cap all Office Supplies discounts at 10%.

**R-O2: Use Office Supplies as a Customer Retention Tool Differently**
Instead of price discounts, use Office Supplies for:
- **Loyalty bundles**: "Buy 3 reams of paper, get a stapler at cost price"
- **Subscription pricing**: Fixed monthly supply packages for Corporate customers (predictable revenue, no discounting)

**R-O3: Grow Office Supplies as a Share of Revenue**
At 50% margin vs. 43–44% for the other categories, every additional FCFA of Office Supplies revenue is worth more to the bottom line. Consider:
- Expanding the product catalog (more SKUs in Paper & Ink)
- Targeting Home Office segment specifically with supply kits
- Marketing campaigns positioning NorthPeak as the go-to B2B supplies vendor
