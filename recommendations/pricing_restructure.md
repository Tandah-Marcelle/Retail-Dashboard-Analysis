## 1. The Pricing Problem in Plain Terms

NorthPeak's standard unit prices are set as fixed markups over cost:

| Product | Unit Cost (FCFA) | Unit Price (FCFA) | Gross Margin (full price) |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Enterprise Laptop | 250,000 | 480,000 | 47.9% |
| 4K Monitor | 90,000 | 175,000 | 48.6% |
| Ergonomic Chair | 45,000 | 95,000 | 52.6% |
| Executive Desk | 85,000 | 180,000 | 52.8% |
| Standing Desk Converter | 30,000 | 65,000 | 53.8% |
| Wireless Headset | 15,000 | 38,000 | 60.5% |
| A4 Paper Box | 12,000 | 22,000 | 45.5% |
| Toner Cartridge | 20,000 | 45,000 | 55.6% |
| Stapler & Binder | 5,000 | 12,000 | 58.3% |

These are healthy margins at full price. The problem is that **discounts are reducing realized prices without any structured pricing logic behind them.** The business is effectively operating multiple implicit price points (full price, 10% off, 20% off, 30% off) — but none of these are intentionally designed, measured, or tied to business outcomes.

## 2. The Pricing Architecture Recommendation

The goal is to replace the current single-price + ad-hoc-discount model with a **three-tier structured pricing architecture.**

### 2.1 The Three-Tier Model

```
TIER 1: LIST PRICE (Standard Consumer)
  ├── Who: Walk-in consumer customers, online purchases
  ├── Price: Current standard price (no change)
  └── Discount: 0% – 5% maximum (seasonal only)

TIER 2: VOLUME / LOYALTY PRICE (Repeat & Bulk Buyers)
  ├── Who: Returning customers, orders of 5+ units
  ├── Price: 8% – 10% below list price (pre-published)
  └── Discount: Fixed — not negotiable at point of sale

TIER 3: CONTRACT PRICE (Corporate Accounts)
  ├── Who: Corporate customers with signed annual agreement
  ├── Minimum annual spend: 5 million FCFA
  ├── Price: 10% – 15% below list price (negotiated upfront)
  └── Discount: Fixed in contract — no further discounting allowed
```

This replaces the current invisible, random discount system with three published price points that salespeople present — they no longer negotiate from scratch on every transaction.

## 3. Product-Level Pricing Recommendations

### 3.1 Enterprise Laptop 15" (PRD-201) — Current price: 480,000 FCFA

**Issue:** Highest absolute discount amounts in the dataset. High-value item attracts aggressive deal-making.

**Recommendation:**
- Do not reduce the list price
- Introduce a **certified refurbished tier** at 350,000 FCFA (if applicable) to address price-sensitive buyers without discounting new units
- For Corporate Tier 3: Laptop price fixed at **432,000 FCFA (10% off)**. No further discounting.
- Create a **Productivity Bundle:** Laptop + Headset (PRD-203) for 495,000 FCFA (vs. 518,000 FCFA individually) — drives headset attachment, absorbs the discount desire without reducing laptop margin

```
Bundle economics:
  Laptop cost: 250,000
  Headset cost: 15,000
  Total cost: 265,000
  Bundle price: 495,000
  Bundle margin: 46.5%  ← Protected vs. 30%+ discounted single laptop
```

---

### 3.2 4K Monitor (PRD-202) — Current price: 175,000 FCFA

**Issue:** Often sold alongside laptops — discount stacking is likely.

**Recommendation:**
- Set a **combo discount ceiling:** Laptop + Monitor purchased together = maximum 12% total discount on Monitor (the lower-price item). Laptop gets 0% additional discount.
- Introduce a **Monitor Subscription / Swap Program** for Corporate customers: after 3 years, they can upgrade to the next model at a reduced rate. This creates retention and predictable revenue without price cutting.

---

### 3.3 Ergonomic Chair (PRD-101) — Current price: 95,000 FCFA

**Issue:** High-volume product, frequently discounted.

**Recommendation:**
- Introduce **quantity-based published pricing:**
  - 1–2 chairs: 95,000 FCFA (list)
  - 3–5 chairs: 88,000 FCFA (published 7% volume price)
  - 6+ chairs: 83,000 FCFA (published 13% corporate price)
- Sales reps present these tiers — they stop negotiating. The customer feels they earned a better price, the company controls the margin.

```
At 6 chairs:
  Revenue: 498,000 FCFA
  Cost:    270,000 FCFA
  Profit:  228,000 FCFA
  Margin:  45.8%  ← Still healthy at "discounted" published price
```

---

### 3.4 Office Supplies (PRD-301, 302, 303) — Paper, Toner, Stapler

**Issue:** Over-discounting low-margin products (Paper at 22,000 FCFA per box needs no incentive discount).

**Recommendation:**
- **Subscription model:** Corporate customers commit to monthly supply deliveries (e.g., 10 reams paper + 2 toner cartridges per month) at list price minus 5%.
- Remove all promotional discounting from Office Supplies entirely. If competitors undercut, compete on delivery reliability and service, not price.
- Consider bundling Stapler & Binder as a **new employee onboarding kit** sold to Corporate HR departments.

## 4. Dynamic vs. Fixed Pricing Considerations

**Should NorthPeak implement dynamic pricing?** (i.e., adjusting prices in real time based on demand)

**Analysis:** At NorthPeak's current scale and market, the answer is **no — not yet.**

Dynamic pricing requires:
- Real-time inventory data linked to sales
- High transaction volume per SKU (NorthPeak has 9 products)
- Customer price tolerance data

What NorthPeak should implement instead is **structured rule-based pricing** (the three-tier model above) which achieves 80% of the benefit with 20% of the complexity.

Revisit dynamic pricing consideration when:
- Product catalog expands to 30+ SKUs
- Online channel accounts for >30% of transactions
- A proper CRM with customer purchase history is in place

## 5. Price Increase Feasibility Assessment

Is it feasible to increase standard prices to offset margin erosion?

| Product | Price Increase Feasibility | Notes |
|---------|--------------------------|-------|
| Enterprise Laptop | 🟡 Moderate | Market-pegged product; a 5–8% increase may be feasible if positioned as premium |
| 4K Monitor | 🟡 Moderate | Similar to laptops — tech pricing is competitive |
| Ergonomic Chair | 🟢 High | Quality/comfort narrative supports premium positioning in Cameroon's growing corporate market |
| Executive Desk | 🟢 High | Furniture is differentiated by quality — less commodity pricing pressure |
| Office Supplies | 🔴 Low | Highly commoditized; price increases risk customer defection |

**Overall recommendation:** A **5% list price increase on Furniture** products (Chair and Desk) is feasible and could recover ~0.5 percentage points of margin without changing discount policy. This should be tested in Tier 1 regions first (Q1 2027) before a national rollout.

## 6. Pricing Governance — What Needs to Change Operationally

The data analysis reveals that the pricing problem is not just about the numbers — it is about **the absence of pricing governance.** The following infrastructure changes are needed:

| Change | Description | Priority |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Publish a Price List | A formal, versioned price list document for all 3 tiers and all 9 products. Updated quarterly. | Critical |
| POS Discount Limits | Configure the sales system to prevent discounts beyond tier ceilings without manager override | Critical |
| Discount Reason Codes | Require sales reps to select a reason code when applying any discount (Loyalty / Volume / Promotion / Clearance) | High |
| Monthly Pricing Review | Finance and Sales review average realized price vs. list price monthly | High |
| Quarterly Price List Review | Review whether list prices need adjustment based on cost changes or competitor pricing | Medium |
| Discount Performance Dashboard | Add a "Price Realization %" metric to the Power BI dashboard: `net_revenue / gross_revenue` | High |
