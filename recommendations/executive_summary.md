# Executive Summary Report
## NorthPeak Retail — Profit Margin Crisis Analysis

**Prepared by:** TANDAH DJIMELI MARCELLE  
**Period Analyzed:** January 2024 – September 2026  
**Report Date:** October 2026  
**Classification:** Strategic — For Leadership Review

---

## The One-Line Finding

> NorthPeak generated 43.05 billion FCFA in revenue over 30 months — but a systematic, uncapped discount policy quietly eroded profit margins by 3.33 percentage points, costing the business an estimated **4.2 billion FCFA in recoverable profit**.

---

## What the Data Shows

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 (Jan–Sep) | Change |
|--------|------|------|----------------|--------|
| Avg Profit Margin % | ~50% | ~46% | ~40% | **-10 pp** |
| Avg Discount Rate | ~6% | ~10% | ~18% | **+12 pp** |
| Revenue | Stable | Stable | Stable | Flat |
| Net Profit | High | Declining | Critically low | **-20%+** |

Revenue stayed flat. Costs stayed flat. The only variable that moved — **discounts** — explains 87% of the margin variance (r = -0.87).

This is not a revenue problem. It is a **pricing discipline problem.**

---

## The Three Critical Alerts

### 🔴 Alert 1 — Margin Has Fallen Below the 45% Internal Target
As of Q3 2026, the company-wide average margin sits at **40.62%** — 4.38 percentage points below the strategic baseline of 45%. The target was breached in Q1 2025 and has not been recovered since. Without intervention, the trajectory points toward a sub-35% margin by mid-2027.

### 🔴 Alert 2 — Nearly 1 in 5 Orders is Being Sold at a Destructive Discount
**19.11% of all transactions (13,377 orders)** carried discounts above 20%. At that level, unit margins on Furniture and Technology compress to 12% or below — barely above cost. Volume did not compensate. Quantity sold has zero correlation (0.00) with discount rate, meaning bulk buyers are not receiving these discounts — they are being applied indiscriminately.

### 🟡 Alert 3 — Two Categories are Carrying a Disproportionate Burden
Furniture and Technology together account for the majority of discount expenditure. Office Supplies, which requires no deep discounting, maintains a ~50% margin and is subsidizing the overall average. If Furniture and Technology margins are not corrected, the drag will eventually pull the entire portfolio below acceptable thresholds.

## What Leadership Must Decide

Three strategic choices are on the table. This report recommends pursuing all three simultaneously:

**1. Implement a Hard Discount Cap at 15%**
No transaction should be authorized above 15% unless approved by a finance officer. Data shows profitability collapses rapidly above this threshold.

**2. Audit and Restructure the Discount Authorization Process**
13,377 high-discount orders happened without generating compensating volume. Someone authorized these. The process needs controls.

**3. Re-price or Re-position Furniture and Technology**
If market competition requires discounts above 15% to close sales, the standard prices are set too high — or the cost structure needs renegotiation with suppliers.

---

## Bottom Line

NorthPeak is not in crisis — yet. With 40.6% margin and 43 billion FCFA in revenue, the business is still profitable. But the direction is wrong, the speed of decline accelerated in 2026, and the cause is fully identified. The data gives leadership a rare gift: **a clear, solvable problem with a measurable fix.**
